Health Effects of Air Pollution

  • Lungs: Cough, wheeze, asthma attacks, reduced lung growth in children.
  • Heart: Higher risk of heart attack, stroke, high blood pressure.
  • Brain: Headache, fatigue, reduced concentration; long-term links to dementia.
  • Pregnancy: Low birth weight, preterm birth, complications.
  • Children & Elderly: More hospital visits, more days of illness.

CO₂, Fossil Fuels & Climate Change

The same activities that cause local air pollution—burning diesel, petrol, coal, and waste—also release carbon dioxide (CO₂), a greenhouse gas that drives climate change.

  • More CO₂ → Warming planet: Heatwaves, heavy rainfall, floods, and sea-level rise.
  • Heat + Pollution: Hot days trap pollutants near the ground, making breathing even harder.
  • Co-benefit: Cutting fossil fuel use improves both health now and climate in future.

What Everyone Can Do

  • Travel: Walk, cycle, or use public transport when possible; share rides.
  • At Home: Avoid burning waste, wood, or plastic; use LPG or clean cooking options.
  • Energy: Switch off lights and fans when not needed; support rooftop solar.
  • Green Spaces: Plant and protect trees; support urban green corridors.
  • Community: Ask schools, offices, and local leaders to monitor AQI and act on bad-air days.

Cleaner air = fewer hospital visits, healthier children, and a safer climate for Kochi.
