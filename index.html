Breath Kochi – Air Quality Insights

By Amala Xavier • Aerosol & Inhalation Scientist

Why This Matters

Kochi’s air contains microscopic particles like PM2.5 that affect lungs, heart, brain, and long‑term health. This page gives clear, science‑based guidance using live AQI data and concise explanations.

Live AQI – Ernakulam

What is PM2.5?

PM2.5 are fine particles smaller than 2.5 micrometers — about 30× thinner than a human hair. They travel deep into the lungs and can enter the bloodstream.

Health Impacts

  • Lungs: asthma, reduced lung growth
  • Heart: stroke, hypertension
  • Brain: fatigue, cognitive decline
  • Pregnancy: low birth weight
  • Children & elderly: higher vulnerability

What You Can Do

  • Use public transport or carpool
  • Avoid burning waste
  • Maintain vehicles
  • Use HEPA filters at home
  • Use HEPA cabin filters in cars
  • Check AQI before outdoor activity
  • Wear N95 masks on bad days

Future Vision for Kochi

  • More green corridors
  • Cleaner public transport
  • Cycling & walking infrastructure
  • Emission‑controlled industries
  • More AQI monitoring stations
